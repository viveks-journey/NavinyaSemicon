Our Approach

A Focused Cross-Border Platform

India imports over 85% of its semiconductor requirements. Closing that gap demands more than capital — it requires sustained knowledge transfer, supply chain integration, and credible industrial partnerships rooted in engineering reality.

Navinya Semicon is an early-stage initiative founded in Germany to build exactly that bridge. We are not a fund or a conglomerate. We are an ecosystem connector — identifying where European semiconductor expertise, process discipline, and supply chain resilience can create concrete value in India's emerging ATMP, design, and materials landscape.

Our starting point is honest: we are in discovery and partnership-formation mode. We engage seriously with executives, policymakers, and technologists on both sides who share the conviction that Indo-German industrial collaboration is a structurally sound bet.

Supply Chain Resilience

European chipmakers are actively de-risking single-source dependencies. India's upstream materials base and ATMP build-out offer a credible diversification path — if the right partnerships are in place.

German Engineering Anchor

Founded and operated from Germany, Navinya Semicon brings direct access to European process know-how, equipment ecosystems, and the network of companies already navigating India market entry.

India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)

₹76,000 crore in government-backed incentives — including 50% capex support for ATMP and fab facilities — creates a policy window that rewards early movers with serious industrial intent.

Indo-German Industrial Alignment

Germany and India share a bilateral technology partnership with growing focus on critical technologies. Navinya Semicon is positioned at that intersection — practical, specific, and long-term.

Where We Focus

Six Coherent Entry Points

Rather than spanning the entire value chain, Navinya Semicon concentrates on six areas where Indo-German collaboration produces the most concrete near-term value.

🧱

Upstream Materials & Substrates

Mapping India's emerging supply base for silicon wafers, specialty chemicals, and compound semiconductor substrates — and connecting European qualification teams with Indian producers.

📦

ATMP, Packaging & Power Electronics

India's highest-readiness semiconductor segment. We track ISM-backed ATMP/OSAT facility development and facilitate technology and process partnerships with European packaging specialists.

⚙️

Fab Digitalization & Supply Chain Software

Applying enterprise software and Industry 4.0 experience to semiconductor manufacturing operations in India — an underserved gap where European process discipline has clear transferable value.

🔬

Chip Design & IP

India has depth in VLSI design talent. We identify collaboration points between Indian design houses and European IP providers, EDA ecosystems, and verification specialists.

🤝

Cross-Border Partnerships

Facilitating specific, well-scoped partnerships between European semiconductor firms and Indian counterparts — from initial market assessment through to MoU and pilot project structuring.

🎓

Indo-German Talent & R&D

Building structured knowledge bridges between IITs, German universities (TU Munich, KIT, TU Dresden), and industry — internship pipelines, joint research, and dual-degree frameworks.

The Structural Case

Why India, Why Now

India's semiconductor ambition is backed by serious policy. The India Semiconductor Mission has committed ₹76,000 crore with 50% capex support for ATMP and fab facilities — one of the most substantial government interventions in the sector globally. This is not speculative; approvals are live and construction has begun.

For European semiconductor companies evaluating supply chain resilience and market diversification, India offers a combination rarely found in a single geography: deep engineering talent, an improving policy environment, domestic demand growth, and genuine government urgency to close the technology gap.

  • 🇮🇳

    ISM: 50% Capex Support for ATMP & Fabs

    ₹76,000 crore (~$9B) committed under the India Semiconductor Mission. Active approvals for ATMP facilities are underway as of 2024.

  • 🏭

    ATMP/OSAT: India's Near-Term Reality

    Advanced packaging and test is where India's semiconductor build-out begins. Multiple facilities under ISM approval present concrete near-term partnership entry points.

  • 👩‍💻

    1.5M+ Engineering Graduates Annually

    India produces the world's largest cohort of STEM graduates. VLSI, embedded systems, and EDA expertise is concentrated in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai.

  • 🌐

    Supply Chain Diversification Pressure

    European chipmakers face regulatory and strategic pressure to reduce single-region supply exposure. India — with its scale, language capability, and legal framework — is a natural counterweight.

  • 🇩🇪

    Indo-German Technology Partnership

    Germany and India have an active bilateral technology collaboration framework. Semiconductor and critical minerals were explicitly named as priority areas in the 2023 IGC joint statement.
