Our Vision

Building India's Semiconductor Sovereignty

India imports over 85% of its semiconductor requirements. With a booming electronics market, a massive engineering talent pool, and unprecedented government backing, the time to act is now.

Navinya Semicon is founded in Germany to combine the precision and credibility of European deep-tech with the ambition and scale of the Indian market — inspired by the trailblazing path of visionaries like Anil Agarwal.

We work across the full semiconductor value chain: from materials and design to manufacturing, packaging, and testing — connecting global expertise with India's strategic needs.

India Semiconductor Mission

Leveraging ₹76,000 crore government incentives under the ISM to accelerate domestic semiconductor capacity.

German Engineering Hub

Operating from Germany to access Europe's semiconductor ecosystem, R&D centers, and global supply chains.

Full Value Chain Focus

Engaging investors, government bodies, and industry partners across design, fab, ATMP, and materials.

Long-term Nation Building

Beyond business — contributing to India's strategic self-reliance in critical technology infrastructure.

The Opportunity

Why India, Why Now

India stands at an inflection point. With a $300B+ electronics market projected by 2026, a digitally-driven economy, and a government committed to making India a global semiconductor hub, the conditions for investment have never been more compelling.

The India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) offers up to 50% capital expenditure support for fabs, ATMP units, and compound semiconductor facilities — a scale of subsidy rarely seen globally.

  • 🇮🇳

    India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)

    ₹76,000 crore (~$10B) scheme offering 50% capex subsidy for semiconductor fabs and ATMP facilities.

  • 📈

    Fastest Growing Market

    India's semiconductor market growing at 19% CAGR, driven by mobile, automotive, and industrial demand.

  • 👩‍💻

    World's Largest STEM Talent Pool

    Over 1.5 million engineering graduates annually — a ready workforce for semiconductor design and manufacturing.

  • 🌐

    Geopolitical Tailwinds

    Global supply chain diversification away from China makes India a strategic alternative for semiconductor production.

  • 🏛️

    Policy & Infrastructure Support

    Dedicated semiconductor zones, single-window clearances, and bilateral agreements with US, Japan, and EU.
